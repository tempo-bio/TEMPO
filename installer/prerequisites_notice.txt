TEMPO-C
Hardware & Driver Prerequisites
===================================================================

TEMPO-C connects to standard USB/UVC cameras and compatible DirectShow capture devices.

For high-speed scientific microscope cameras:
1. Use the installed driver for your camera, or install the matching vendor driver:
   - Hamamatsu: DCAM-API
   - Teledyne FLIR: Spinnaker SDK
   - Photometrics: PVCAM
   - Basler: Pylon Camera Software Suite
2. For Micro-Manager devices such as shutters, filter wheels, and stages, use
   your existing Micro-Manager installation and hardware configuration. On a
   new computer, install Micro-Manager 2.0 and the adapters for your devices.

After installation, open Camera Settings in Acquisition to configure
your camera.
