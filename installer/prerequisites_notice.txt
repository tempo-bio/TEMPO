TEMPO
Hardware & Driver Prerequisites
===================================================================

TEMPO connects natively to standard USB/UVC webcams out of the box.

For high-speed scientific microscope cameras:
1. Ensure your camera's vendor SDK/device driver is installed on Windows:
   - Hamamatsu: DCAM-API
   - Teledyne FLIR: Spinnaker SDK
   - Photometrics: PVCAM
   - Basler: Pylon Camera Software Suite
2. If using Micro-Manager hardware devices (shutters, filter wheels, stages),
   ensure Micro-Manager 2.0 gamma is installed in standard paths:
   (e.g., C:\Program Files\Micro-Manager-2.0gamma)

You can configure and test your camera connection anytime inside TEMPO
under the Acquisition tab or using the Guided Setup Wizard.
